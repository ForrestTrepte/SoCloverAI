We are creating an expansion set of keyword cards for the game So Clover!

In So Clover, players create one-word clues connecting pairs of keywords.

Rate the following words from 1 (worst) to 10 (best) based on how suitable they are for the game in the following aspects:

# Association Diversity

How many different ideas, concepts, domains, or topics the word naturally connects to. Prefer words that support many independent associations in different domains rather than many very similar associations.

High ratings:

connect broadly across many concepts
participate in many semantic neighborhoods
naturally combine with many other words

Low ratings:

isolated or narrow
few meaningful associations
difficult to connect naturally

Provide a rating for each category of association below:

## Semantic/Category

Definition:
  Associations based on meaning, taxonomy, properties, or conceptual similarity. These are the most straightforward "related concept" connections.

Examples:
- dog → animal, pet, bark, leash
- apple → fruit, food, tree
- ocean → water, fish, wave

## Functional

Definition:
  Associations based on what something does, how it is used, or what it interacts with.

Examples:
- hammer → building, nails, wood
- key → lock, open
- oven → bake, rack

## Multiple Meanings

Definition:
  Associations arising because the word has multiple meanings or interpretations.
  
Examples:
- pitch → melody, baseball, sales, tar
- match → competition, fire, pair

## Metaphorical/Symbolic

Definition:
  Associations where the word represents broader ideas, concepts, emotions, or relationships beyond its literal meaning. These associations may arise from figurative analogy, abstract comparison, or culturally recognized symbolic meaning.

Examples:
- fire → passion, energy, intensity
- anchor → emotional stability
- bridge → connection between people
- dove → peace
- heart → love
- scales → justice

## Idioms/Phrases

Definition:
  Associations arising from common expressions or fixed phrases.

Examples:
- ice → break (the ice)
- glass → (glass) ceiling

## Wordplay

Definition:
  Associations based on pronunciation, rhyme, puns, spelling, alliteration, or linguistic play rather than meaning.

Examples:
- knight → night
- star → super, fish
- desert → dessert

## Visual

Definition:
  Associations based on what the word visually evokes. These often rely on mental pictures, shapes, colors, scenes, or aesthetics.

Examples:
- pyramid → triangular structure
- jungle → dense green vegetation
- mirror → shiny reflection

## Emotional

Definition:
  Associations tied to feelings, moods, or emotional tone.

Examples:
- funeral → sadness
- birthday → joy
- home → comfort

## Cultural/Historical

Definition:
  Associations arising from shared media, pop culture, religion, memes, brands, folklore, societal knowledge, historical events, people, or movements.

Examples:
- apple → technology, company, phone
- romeo → romance
- matrix → trinity, pill, AI
- titanic → iceberg, lifeboat
- berlin → east, west, wall
- caesar → rome, emperor, march
- revolution → French, American

## Overall Associations

Definition:
  Overall breadth, diversity, and flexibility of the word's associations across different types of clueing routes.

# Gameplay Suitability

Provide ratings for the following dimensions of gameplay potential:

## Recognizability

Definition:
  How likely the average player is to know and understand the word quickly.

## Inoffensiveness

Definition:
  How likely the average player is not to be offended by the word. Please provide ratings for offensive words, but give them a low rating for inoffensiveness, to ensure that they are excluded from the expansion.

## Evocativeness

Definition:
  How strongly the word evokes mental imagery, sensory experiences, emotions, stories, memorable concepts, or vivid situations.

## Fun

Definition:
  Overall potential for creating clever moments, surprising connections, memorable gameplay, satisfying social interactions, or interesting player decisions.

## Overall Gameplay

Definition:
  Overall rating of Gameplay Suitability.

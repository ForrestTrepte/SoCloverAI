We are creating an expansion set of keyword cards for the game So Clover!

In So Clover, players create one-word clues connecting pairs of keywords.

Rate the following words from 1 (worst) to 10 (best) based on how suitable they are for the game in the following aspects:

# Association Breadth

How many different ideas, concepts, domains, or topics the word naturally connects to.

High ratings:

connect broadly across many concepts
participate in many semantic neighborhoods
naturally combine with many other words

Low ratings:

isolated or narrow
few meaningful associations
difficult to connect naturally

Provide the following ratings:
* semantic/category relationships: Associations based on meaning, taxonomy, properties, or conceptual similarity. These are the most straightforward "related concept" connections. Examples: dog → animal, pet, bark, leash; apple → fruit, food, tree; ocean → water, fish, wave
* metaphorical meanings: Associations where the word is used figuratively rather than literally. The connection depends on analogy or abstract comparison. Examples: fire → passion, energy, intensity; anchor → emotional stability; bridge → connection between people
* symbolic meanings: Associations where the word conventionally represents a broader idea or concept. Examples: dove → peace; heart → love; scales → justice
* idioms/phrases: Associations arising from common expressions or fixed phrases. Examples: ice → break (the ice); glass → (glass) ceiling




The best keywords connect to many different ideas. Consider multiple kinds of association possibilities, including:
* semantic/category relationships
* idioms and phrases
* visual imagery
* emotions
* physical objects or actions
* wordplay
* cultural or historical references
* multiple interpretations or senses of the word

Provide a single overall rating for each word based on the diversity of its associations.

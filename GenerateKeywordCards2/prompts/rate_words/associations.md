We are creating an expansion set of keyword cards for the game So Clover!

In So Clover, players create one-word clues connecting pairs of keywords.

Rate the following words from 1 (worst) to 10 (best) based on how suitable they are for the game.

The best keywords connect to many different ideas. Consider multiple kinds of association possibilities, including:
* semantic/category relationships
* metaphorical and symbolic meanings
* idioms and phrases
* visual imagery
* emotions
* physical objects or actions
* wordplay
* cultural or historical references
* multiple interpretations or senses of the word

Provide a single overall rating for each word based on the diversity of its associations.

We are creating an expansion set of keyword cards for the game So Clover!

In So Clover, players create one-word clues connecting pairs of keywords.

Rate the following words from 1 (worst) to 10 (best) based on how suitable they are for the game.

Strong keywords:
* naturally connect to many different ideas
* support many distinct kinds of associations and clueing routes
* work well with a wide variety of partner words
* allow creative, clever, and satisfying clue combinations
* are recognizable and accessible to most players
* evoke strong imagery, concepts, emotions, stories, or cultural associations
* support both clue-giving and clue-solving gameplay

Weak keywords are:
* overly generic
* overly narrow or obscure
* difficult to connect meaningfully
* repetitive in the kinds of clues they support
* likely to create weak or frustrating gameplay

Provide a single overall rating for each word based on the overall gameplay potential it would create in So Clover.
